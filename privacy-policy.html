Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

RentEasy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

📌 Quick Summary: We collect only the data needed to connect tenants, landlords, and managers. We never sell your data. You can request deletion anytime.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile photo
  • Property Information: Property details, photos, location, and pricing when you list a property
  • Communication: Messages, chat history, and correspondence with other users
  • Transaction Data: Rental agreements, commission payments, and verification records
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect your location to show nearby properties and connect you with local managers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Connect tenants with landlords and verified managers
  • Verify property listings and ensure their accuracy
  • Process rental transactions and commission payments
  • Send you important updates about your listings or inquiries
  • Improve our app and website functionality
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information in the following cases:

  • With Property Managers: When you list a property, your contact details are shared with managers who accept the listing
  • With Landlords/Tenants: To facilitate property viewings and rental agreements
  • Service Providers: We use Supabase for database hosting and authentication, and Facebook for login
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

4. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion at any time through our Delete Account page.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access – Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct – Update inaccurate information
  • Delete – Request permanent account deletion
  • Withdraw Consent – Opt out of marketing communications

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We use Supabase's built-in security features, including Row Level Security (RLS) and encrypted connections.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase – Database, authentication, and storage
  • Facebook Login – For authentication
  • Google Cloud – For hosting and services
  • Resend – For transactional emails

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and to remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect app functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

RentEasy is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
