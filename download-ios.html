How to add RentEasy to your Home Screen

1

Open the link in Safari — Chrome and other browsers won't show the "Add to Home Screen" option on iOS.

2

Tap "Open RentEasy Web App" above to load the app.

3

Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen.

4

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."

5

Edit the name if you like, then tap "Add" in the top right.

6

The RentEasy icon now appears on your Home Screen — tap it to open the app!

✅ The web app works offline, updates automatically, and doesn't require an App Store download.

How to sign up

1

Open RentEasy from your Home Screen icon (or Safari) and tap Sign Up.

2

Enter your phone number or email and verify the code sent to you.

3

Choose the role that matches you — see what each one does below.

4

Complete your profile (ID/KYC is required for Landlords, Managers, Estate Firms & Service Providers).

5

Start browsing, listing, or managing — RentEasy takes it from here!
